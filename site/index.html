Know you're heard.

Ciao is the easiest way to talk to your computer. Your words appear while you are still speaking, so you see what it heard — and fix the one word that flips the meaning before it lands in your prompt.

Windows · free and open source · Russian and English · uses your OpenAI API key

  • Live, not after the fact New words fade in bright and settle to grey, about 0.8 s after you say them.
  • Hold a key, or just say "ciao" Push to talk on Right Ctrl, or start hands-free with your voice.
  • One of the best speech models Ciao listens with OpenAI's gpt-live-transcribe, one of the best voice-to-text models out there. It runs on your own OpenAI API key: about $1 per hour of talking, nothing while idle.
  • Pastes where you started The text lands in the window you were typing in. Nothing is lost if the network drops.
